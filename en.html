Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 27, 2026

This app ("App") values your privacy and follows the policy below.

1. Information Collected

The App stores your income and expense data locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to the developer’s server or any external server.

2. Local Data Storage

Transaction records, categories, and app settings entered in the App are stored only on the user's device. The developer does not access or collect the user's financial records.

3. Ads and Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect data such as device information, advertising identifiers, and app usage information for ad delivery, ad performance measurement, and fraud prevention.

For more details, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy and advertising policies.

4. GDPR and CCPA Notice

Users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or California, USA may have rights under applicable privacy laws, including the right to access, delete, restrict processing, or opt out of certain data collection.

The App itself does not transmit the user's expense-tracking data externally. However, third-party services such as Google AdMob may process data for advertising purposes. Users can manage ad personalization and related data processing through Google's settings and policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All user data is stored on the user's device. Users can delete stored data through the App's delete functions or by uninstalling the App.

6. Security

The App applies reasonable security measures to protect user data. However, risks related to the user's device environment, such as device loss, rooting, or malicious apps, must be managed by the user.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated due to changes in laws, service features, or advertising policies. Any changes will be posted on this page.
